vehicle heading: estimator vs android native bearing

At each sample along the drive, two arrows share one position. The orange arrow is the drive-mode estimator (GNSS course while moving, gyro hold at stops). The blue arrow is the Android rotation-vector heading, which includes the magnetometer and the phone's ~45° mount rotation. The orange arrows should point down the road; the blue arrows show what image bearings would look like if we trusted the native compass.
